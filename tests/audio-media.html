Prepare a score, then press Play. Music goes directly through the normal audio player. Preparation can take time on a phone. No live Web Audio runs unless you enable the comparison sounds.

Listen for crackling with music alone, then with hum and repeated clicks. Check pause/resume,
mute, changing scores and at least one complete loop. You can seek near the end with the player controls.
Changing scores stops the old one; the replacement needs preparation and a new Play gesture.
Each loop repeats the rendered percussion noise and has a 3 ms edge taper. Listen for a gap or click at the seam.
The rendered music and live comparison sounds have separate output compression, so their combined mix can differ from the app.
